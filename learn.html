Trading basics

What is a stock?

A stock is a small piece of ownership in a company. If the company does well, the price of your share usually rises. If it struggles, the price can fall.

Buying and selling

You buy a share at the current price and sell it later at the price then. If the selling price is higher than what you paid, you make a profit. If it's lower, you take a loss.

What does "Today" mean in the market table?

It's the percentage the price has moved since you opened the page. Green means it's up, red means it's down.

Average cost

If you buy the same stock at different prices, your average cost is the total you paid divided by the number of shares. Your profit or loss is measured against this number.

Portfolio value

Your portfolio value is your cash plus the current value of every stock you hold. It changes whenever prices move, even if you don't trade.

Diversification

Spreading money across several stocks means one bad stock hurts less. It's one of the simplest ways to reduce risk.

This is practice only

TradeSphere uses virtual money and simulated prices. Real markets are less predictable, so treat this as a place to learn, not a guide to real investing.
